01 — The Challenge

Making the airport experience more youthful and independent

Travelers face significant stress navigating Boston Logan Airport, particularly the "curb-to-gate" journey. For elderly travelers, this experience is often marred by expansive terminals, confusing signage, and physical exhaustion.

Our team, WiseWanderers, set out to transform this journey into a smooth, welcoming, and exciting experience that proves adventure has no age limit.

02 — User Research

Understanding the barriers to travel

"Airports are expansive and I have joint problems... carrying bags is an issue. Assistant carts are on weird intervals which prevent me from getting help."

Through interviews and empathy mapping, we identified critical friction points for elderly users:

  • Cognitive Load: Dyslexia and noise interference make reading gate numbers and announcements difficult.
  • Physical Strain: Long TSA lines and vast distances between gates cause lightheadedness and pain.
  • Language Barriers: Limited English proficiency creates a fear of getting lost or misunderstood.
  • Tech Adoption: A preference for simple, high-contrast, and large-text interfaces.

03 — Meet Nhung

Mrs. Nhung Trường, 72

Goal: Travel to see grandkids without fear or exhaustion.

Nhung suffers from right-side hemiparesis and tremors. She needs ultra-simple guidance that guarantees safe, escorted travel with wheelchair priority and no language barriers.

Needs

Step-by-step guidance, minimal decision making, and independent reach.

Frustrations

Fear of falling, crowded environments, and dependence on others.

04 — The Solution

Explorer Elite: A Companion Service

We designed a multi-faceted service centered around a companion app and physical airport infrastructure improvements.

1

Simplified Wayfinding

Floor-level, color-coded signage and a simplified in-app map that shows exact paths to gates and amenities.

2

Pre-Requested Assistance

One-tap booking for wheelchairs, luggage assistance, and priority TSA lanes directly through the app.

3

Loyalty & Rewards

A "Traveler Points" system that rewards users for using the service, redeemable for food discounts and lounge access.

4

Accessibility Mode

High-contrast UI, large text (Atkinson Hyperlegible), and voice-guided check-in for low-vision and low-tech users.

05 — Design Specs
